Spotlight is for the one thing you most want people to notice: a baptism Sunday, a new sermon series, the fall retreat. It’s a big photo or short silent video with a title, and then a button, a form or nothing.

Set it up

  1. Add the blockClick + Add block → ✨ Spotlight.
  2. Write the wordsSmall text above the title (like “This Sunday”), the Title, an optional Date and time, and a line or two of text.
  3. Add a photo or videoSee below.
  4. Choose what goes underneathA button, A form or Nothing.

Photo or video

Under Photo or video, set Show to:

  • A photo: upload one or paste a link.
  • A video (plays silently on a loop): upload a short clip under 6 MB, or paste a link to an .mp4 file (Dropbox share links work). It plays muted, like a moving photo. A 5 to 10 second clip is plenty. Add a cover photo to show while it loads.

Then frame it:

  • Darken behind the text keeps a white title readable on a bright photo.
  • Zoom, Left / right and Up / down pick the part of the picture that shows.
  • Banner height sets how tall it is.
  • Text placement puts the words Under the photo or On top of the photo.

Underneath: a button or a form

  • A button: set its text and what it opens, like any button.
  • A form: a short sign-up right there, with questions you choose (name, email and phone to start). Answers arrive in the portal with your connect card answers, labeled with the Spotlight’s title.
Video too big?

Trim it shorter, or lower its resolution to 720p, before uploading. Phone video editors and free tools like HandBrake can both do this.
