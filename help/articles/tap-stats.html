Every published page counts its visits and button taps. You can tell which Sunday was busy, whether anyone found the connect card, and which buttons nobody uses.

At a glance

Each page card in the portal shows visits this week, the number of taps, the top button and a small chart of the last 14 days.

The full stats

Click the stats on a page card to open the details:

  • Time range: last 7, 30 or 90 days, or the last year.
  • Visits, button taps and taps per visit, a rough measure of how useful people find the page.
  • A chart of visits per day.
  • Buttons tapped: every button, most-tapped first, named by its label.

The activity log

Under the stats, Activity lists one day at a time, with a chart of the busiest hours. Each visit is a row with its time, followed by the buttons tapped during that visit, in order. Came back means taps from someone who already had the page open. Use ‹ › or pick a date to see another day. Times are in your own time zone, and the log keeps the last 90 days.

What counts

Counted asWhen
A visitSomeone opens the page, from a tag, a QR code, your website or the live link. If they refresh or come back within 30 minutes, it isn’t counted again.
A tapSomeone taps a button, card, banner or link on the page. It’s counted under the label people see, like “Plan a visit”. Copy and Send on invites also name the invite.

Your own visits while testing count too. Use the editor’s preview to check things, which isn’t counted.

Privacy

DoorStep doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t know who visitors are and doesn’t follow them anywhere else. It only keeps daily counts per page and per button, and the activity log’s times, grouped by a random code for each browser tab that says nothing about the person. You don’t need a cookie banner for it.

Tips for useful numbers

  • Give buttons clear, distinct labels. Two buttons both called “Learn more” are counted together.
  • Renaming a button starts a new line in the list under its new name.
  • Every tag and QR code that opens the same page counts toward the same numbers.
