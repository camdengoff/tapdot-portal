A PDF report puts your tap stats on paper: the totals, a chart, your busiest days and buttons, and a row for each page. It’s handy for a board meeting, a monthly staff email or a year-end look back.

Make a report

  1. Click ⤓ PDF reportIt’s at the top of your church’s screen in the portal (on a phone it just says Report). It’s also on a stats link page, and as ⤓ PDF in any page’s tap stats for a report on that page alone.
  2. Pick a time periodLast 7 days, last 30 days, last 3 months, last 12 months, this month so far, last month, this year so far, or Pick dates… for any dates in the past year.
  3. Pick the pagesAll pages, or just one.
  4. Click Download PDFThe file is made right in your browser and saved to your downloads.

What’s in it

  • Visits, button taps, taps per visit and the busiest day, with the change from the same number of days just before (when DoorStep still has them).
  • A chart of visits and taps for each day, or each week for periods longer than three months.
  • Busiest days of the week, so you can see how much Sunday matters.
  • The most-tapped buttons and a table of every page, busiest first.
  • Tick A page for each page to add each page’s own chart and every button tapped.
Connect card answers

Owners and editors can tick Count connect card answers to add how many answers each page got in the period. Only the number goes in the PDF, never what people wrote. People added as Stats only don’t get this option, and a stats link only offers it if the link includes answers.

DoorStep keeps one year of tap stats, so a report can go back up to 365 days.
