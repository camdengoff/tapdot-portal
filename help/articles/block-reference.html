Here’s every block you can add with + Add block, grouped by what it’s for. Blocks with their own guide are linked.

Photos and headers

BlockWhat it’s for
🖼️ HeroA big photo at the top with a title card. Add more than one photo to make a fading slideshow, and set the seconds per photo. The title can sit below the photo, on top of it, or be left off.
✨ SpotlightA photo or silent looping video with a title over it, then a button, a short form or nothing underneath. Great for a baptism Sunday or a big event. Spotlight guide.
🎞️ SlidesPhotos people swipe through, with dots, arrows, autoplay and an optional header. “Slow zoom” gently zooms each photo while it shows.
🏞️ ImageOne photo, with an optional caption and a tap action. Fill in the description so screen readers can describe it.
🧩 Photo gridA grid of 2 to 4 columns. Tapping a photo opens it full screen.
▶️ VideoA YouTube or Vimeo video. Paste the normal link from your browser.

Text

BlockWhat it’s for
🔤 Section headingA title between parts of the page, with an optional link on the right like “See all”.
📝 TextParagraphs. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Can sit in a card or use the dimmer secondary color.
🔽 DropdownsQuestions that open to show the answer, FAQ style. Each can hold a photo and a button. Tick “Only one open at a time” to keep it tidy.

Buttons and links

BlockWhat it’s for
🔘 ButtonsOne or more buttons, as a side-scrolling row, a wrapping row, a grid of 2 or 3, or a full-width stack. Each button has a label, an optional emoji icon and an action. What a button can do.
🪧 Banner cardA wide tappable card with an icon or picture, a small label, a title, a subtitle and an arrow. Good for “New here? Start here”.
🃏 Card rowCards with photos: ministries, staff, upcoming series. Show them side-scrolling, as a grid or as a list, with a small picture on the left or a big one on top.
💌 Invite messagesReady-written invites people copy or send to a friend in one tap. Invite messages guide.

Live lists

BlockWhat it’s for
📰 RSS feedSmall groups, blog posts or sermons straight from your website, kept up to date on their own. RSS feed guide.
📅 Upcoming eventsThe next few events from a Squarespace events page. Upcoming events guide.

Forms and timing

BlockWhat it’s for
✍️ Connect cardA form visitors fill in. Answers show up in the portal. Connect card guide.
⏳ CountdownCounts down to a date and time, then shows a message or hides itself. Countdown guide.

Layout and extras

BlockWhat it’s for
➖ Spacer / dividerExtra space between blocks, with or without a thin line.
🧑‍💻 Custom HTMLPaste embed code from another service, like a map or a sign-up widget. It’s placed on the page as it is, so only paste code from a source you trust.
Tap actions are everywhere

Heroes, images, cards, slides, banners and buttons all have a When tapped setting. They can all open links, pop-ups, files and more, in the same way. See What a button can do.
