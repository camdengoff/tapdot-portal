Undo and redo

Use ↶ and ↷ in the top bar, or Ctrl/⌘ + Z and Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z. Undo covers everything you’ve done since you opened the editor, including deleted blocks and applied templates. When you’re typing in a box, those keys undo your typing instead.

Version history

🕘 History keeps copies of the page you can go back to:

  • Saved: versions you save yourself, with a note.
  • Autosave: one every 10 minutes while you edit, if something changed.
  • One each time you publish.
  • Before restoring: what you had just before opening an older version.

Save a version before a big change

  1. Click 🕘 History
  2. Type a noteSomething you’ll recognize later, like “Before Easter changes”.
  3. Click Save this versionIt goes to the top of the list.

Go back to an earlier version

Click Open next to a version. It replaces what’s in the editor, and Undo brings back what you had. Nothing goes live until you publish, so you can look around safely.

History stays on this computer

Versions are kept in the browser you’re using, not in your church’s account. A teammate on another computer won’t see them, and clearing your browser’s data removes them. For a copy you can keep anywhere, click Save project to download the page as a file, and Open to load it again.

If something went live by mistake

Open an earlier version from History (or fix the mistake), then publish again. The live page updates within about a minute.
