Titles, subtitles, button labels and most text boxes have a small toolbar above them. It works like a word processor: highlight some words, then click a button.

The toolbar

ButtonDoes
A (color)Opens the colors. Pick Accent to use your theme’s accent color, one of the ready-made colors, or 🎨 for any color by its hex code (like #cc4410).
BBold.
IItalic.
🔗Turns the highlighted words into a link. Paste the address when asked.
ClearRemoves formatting from the highlighted words. With nothing highlighted, it clears the whole box.
Use Accent, not a fixed color

Words colored with Accent follow your theme. If you change the accent color later, they change too. Words in a fixed color stay that color.

Highlight a word in a title

The templates color one word of a title, like “Welcome Home”. To do the same, highlight the word, click A and choose Accent.

Paragraphs and line breaks

  • In a Text block, leave a blank line between paragraphs.
  • Press Enter once for a new line inside the same paragraph.
  • Single-line boxes, like button labels, stay on one line.

Emoji

Emoji work anywhere in text. Icons on buttons, banners and menus have their own box with a picker of common church emoji, like 🙏 👋 📅 ☕ and 👶, and you can paste any other.
