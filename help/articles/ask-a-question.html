The Ask a question block is a little chat box. A visitor types something like “do you have a nursery?” or taps a suggested question, and your answer pops up right away. It’s free, works on every phone, and never makes up an answer: it only uses what you write.

Add the block

  1. Add itClick + Add block → 💬 Ask a question. It comes with six common visitor questions ready to edit: service times, kids, parking, what to wear, where you are and watching online.
  2. Fill in your answersOpen each question under Questions and answers and change the answer to fit your church. Answers can use bold, colors and links.
  3. Add a button if it helpsEach answer can have a button under it, like Get directions or Watch live. It works like any other button.
  4. Try it in the previewType a few questions the way a visitor would, and check you get the right answer.

How it finds the answer

Visitors don’t need to type your exact words. DoorStep looks at the important words in what they typed, understands common church words that mean the same thing (nursery and kids, mass and service, livestream and online), allows small typos, and picks the closest question in your list.

Tip

If a question isn’t matching, add a few lines under Other ways people ask, like “When does church start” or “Sunday schedule”. More questions and more ways to ask means better answers.

When there’s no answer

If nothing in your list fits, the visitor sees the message under When there’s no answer. By default it says your team would love to help, with an Email us button. Point that button at your office email or your connect card.

Other settings

  • First message: the chat bubble people see before they ask anything.
  • Suggested questions: how many of your questions show as buttons to tap. Set it to 0 to hide them.
  • Show it: on the page, or in a pop-up opened from any button, banner or hero (choose When tapped → Open one of my pop-up menus or lists, then pick it).
