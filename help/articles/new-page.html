Most churches have a few DoorStep pages: one for the welcome desk, one for seat-back connect cards, and one for whatever event is coming up. Each page has its own install code, stats and answers.

Make the page

  1. Click + New pageIt’s at the top right of your church’s screen.
  2. Name itUse something your team will recognize, like “Welcome desk” or “Fall retreat sign-up”. Only your team sees the name.
  3. Pick a starting pointClick Start from scratch for an empty page, or click a template. Each template shows a live preview you can scroll.
  4. Make it yoursThe editor opens straight away. Templates come with example photos, times and links, so swap in your own before you publish.
Tip

Already have a page that’s close to what you want? Use ••• → Duplicate on its card instead. You keep its colors and blocks.

The starter templates

TemplateGood for
Sunday welcomeThe main welcome desk tag. A welcome photo, quick buttons, service times, a connect card that opens in a pop-up and a friend invite.
Connect cardJust a connect card, for pew tags and seat-back QR codes.
Event sign-upOne event: photo, countdown, details, an FAQ and a sign-up form.
GivingButtons to your own online giving and text-to-give, a mailing address and a giving FAQ. DoorStep doesn’t handle the money; see Link to your online giving.
Link listA simple stack of buttons, like a link-in-bio page.

Your DoorStep contact may add more templates over time. Changes to a template only affect pages made after the change.

What happens next

A new page starts as Not published, so nobody sees it until you’re ready. When it is, follow Put your page on your website once, then Publish.
