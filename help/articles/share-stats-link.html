A stats link is a web page showing your church’s tap stats and nothing else. Anyone with the link can look, with no account or password, and nothing on it can be changed. It’s ideal for a staff meeting, a board report or a pastor who just wants the numbers.

Make the link

  1. Click 📊 Share statsIt’s at the top of your church’s screen in the portal. Only owners see it.
  2. Choose about answersTick Also show connect card answers only if everyone with the link should see names, emails and other details people sent through your forms.
  3. Click Make linkThen Copy link and send it, or ↗ Open to see what they’ll see.

What’s on the stats page

  • Totals and a chart for all your pages together, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days or the last year.
  • Each live page with its visits and top buttons, and a link to see the page.
  • Each page’s details and activity log, the same as in the portal.
  • Connect card answers, only if you ticked the box (read-only: they can’t be deleted from here).

It’s a good one to bookmark: the numbers are always up to date.

Change or turn off the link

Click 📊 Share stats again to:

  • Tick or untick Also show connect card answers. It changes straight away for everyone with the link.
  • New link: makes a fresh link. The old one stops working, so use this if the link went somewhere it shouldn’t.
  • Turn off: stops the link. Anyone who opens it sees that it’s turned off.
Anyone with the link can see it

Treat the link like a key. Share it with the people who need it, and make a new one if it gets passed around.
