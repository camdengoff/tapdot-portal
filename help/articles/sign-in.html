The DoorStep portal is at tapdot.camdengoff.com/app. There’s no public sign-up: someone who manages your church’s pages adds your email first. After that you can sign in two ways.

Sign in with Google

If your email is a Google account (Gmail or Google Workspace), click Sign in with Google and pick that account. You don’t need a password or a link. It only works with the exact email you were added with.

Sign in with a password

To use a password, you first set one from a password link:

  1. Get your linkWhoever added you sends it to you. It works once, for 7 days.
  2. Open it and set a passwordType your name and a password of at least 10 characters, twice, then click Save and sign in.
  3. Sign in next timeUse your email and that password on the sign-in page.
Forgot your password?

If your email is a Google account, you can always use Sign in with Google. Otherwise, ask your DoorStep contact (whoever set DoorStep up for your church) for a new password link. For safety, church owners can only send links to people who haven’t signed in yet.

Your account

Click your name in the top right corner of the portal to:

  • Change name: the name your team sees beside edits and publishes, like “Edited 2 hours ago by Sam”.
  • Change password, or Set a password if you’ve only used Google so far. Changing it signs you out on every other device.
  • Sign out.

You stay signed in on a device for 30 days.

If you can’t get in

  • “You haven’t been added to a church.” You signed in, but your email isn’t on any church yet. Often you were added with a different address than the Google account you picked. Ask whoever added you to check the spelling under People.
  • “This account has no password yet.” Use Sign in with Google, or ask whoever added you for a password link.
  • “This link has expired or was already used.” Ask for a new password link.
  • “Too many tries.” After several wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. Wait and try again.
