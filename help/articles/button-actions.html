Buttons, banners, cards, slides, heroes and images all have a When tapped setting. Pick what should happen, then fill in the boxes that appear under it.

All the choices

When tappedWhat happens
Open a linkGoes to a web address. Tick “Open in a new tab” for sites outside your church’s.
Open page in pop-up sheetOpens a web page in a sheet that slides up over your DoorStep page, so people don’t lose their place. See Pop-up sheets below.
Open one of my pop-up menus or listsOpens a pop-up menu you made, a connect card in a pop-up, or an RSS feed list set to open in a pop-up.
Show a photo or PDF (full screen)Opens a file, like this week’s bulletin, in a full-screen viewer with its own buttons. See Photo and PDF viewer.
Copy text to clipboardCopies text, like the Wi-Fi password or a mailing address, and shows a short message such as “Copied!”.
Call a phone numberStarts a call on a phone.
Send a text messageOpens Messages with your number filled in, plus an optional starting message.
Send an emailOpens a new email to your address, with an optional subject. See Email buttons.
Scroll to a blockJumps down the page to another block. The block needs an anchor name first; see Advanced editing.
Share this pageOpens the phone’s share sheet so people can send your page (or another link) to a friend.
Does nothingFor a card or photo that’s just for looking at.

Pop-up sheets

A pop-up sheet shows another web page, like your prayer request form or giving page, inside a panel over your DoorStep page. People close it and they’re right back where they were.

  • Pop-up title is the heading on the sheet. Leave it blank to use the button’s label.
  • Some websites refuse to be shown inside another page, and appear blank in a sheet. To fix that, set up your own pop-up worker. See Fix blank pop-ups.
  • Until your page has a pop-up worker, pop-up links open in a new tab instead, so they always work.

Email buttons

Email buttons open the visitor’s mail app. Many phones and computers don’t have one set up, so if nothing opens, DoorStep offers Open in Gmail, Open in Outlook and Copy email address instead.

Email addresses are written into your page in a scrambled form that spam robots can’t read, and that website services like Cloudflare don’t rewrite. Visitors don’t notice anything different.

Which taps are counted

Every button tap is counted in tap stats under the button’s label. Short, clear labels like “Plan a visit” make your stats easier to read than “Click here”.
