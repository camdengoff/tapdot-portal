DoorStep doesn’t take, process or store gifts. Your church keeps using its own giving service (for example Tithe.ly, Pushpay, Planning Center Giving, Subsplash or your bank’s), and your DoorStep page sends people there.

Add a Give button

  1. Find your giving linkThe web address of your church’s online giving page. It’s usually on your website’s Give page, or in your giving service’s settings.
  2. Add a buttonIn a Buttons block (or a banner or card), add a button labeled “Give” with an icon like 💵 or ❤️.
  3. Set When tappedChoose Open a link and paste your giving link. Tick Open in a new tab.

Or open it in a pop-up

You can choose Open page in pop-up sheet instead, so people give without leaving your page. Test it on a phone first: many giving services refuse to show inside another page for security, and some ask people to sign in, which works better in a full tab. If in doubt, use Open a link.

Text-to-give

If your giving service has text-to-give, add a button set to Send a text message with its number and starting word, like “Give”.

The Giving template

The Giving starter template has buttons for online giving and text-to-give, a card with your mailing address for checks, and a giving FAQ. Replace the example links with your own. See Make a new page.

See how many people tap Give

Your tap stats count taps on the Give button. DoorStep can’t see whether a gift was made or for how much; that stays with your giving service.
