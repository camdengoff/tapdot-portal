What DoorStep is

A page builder, a publisher and a scoreboard for the moments people walk in.

Put a tag on the welcome desk, a QR code in the bulletin or a sticker on the seat backs. They all open one DoorStep page, and you keep that page fresh from your phone or laptop.

01

Build it visually

Pick a starter template or a blank page, then add blocks: a hero photo, buttons, a live events list, a connect card. Colors, photos and corners are all yours, and nothing needs code.

02

Paste one code block, once

DoorStep gives you two lines to paste into a page on your website. That’s the last time anyone touches your site’s code.

<div class="tapdot-page" data-page="…"></div> <script src="…/embed.js"></script>
03

Publish, and watch it work

Press Publish and the live page updates in seconds. The tags and QR codes never change. Then see how many people visited and what they tapped.

The most helpful parts

Built with a real church, one Sunday at a time.

DoorStep started as the tap tag page for one congregation. Every feature here came from something they needed on a real Sunday.

Tap stats

Know what people actually tap.

Every page shows its visits, taps and most-used buttons, with a chart for the last two weeks. You can tell which Sunday was busy and whether anyone found the giving button.

  • No cookies and no tracking banners. A refresh within 30 minutes isn’t counted twice.
  • Give your pastor or board a read-only stats link, with no sign-in needed.
  • Add people as Stats only, so they can see the numbers without editing anything.

Live lists

Events and small groups that keep themselves up to date.

Point an Upcoming events block at your Squarespace events page, or an RSS feed block at your small groups or blog. When the website changes, the tap page changes with it.

  • Show it as a list, big cards or side-scrolling cards, with category filters.
  • Tuck the whole list behind a banner card or a button, so it opens in a sheet.
  • Each item opens a details sheet with its photo, summary and links.
Connect card

The welcome card, without the pencils.

Add a Connect card block and visitors fill it in on their phone. Answers land on the page’s card in your portal, ready to read or download as a spreadsheet.

  • Text, email, phone, choices and checkboxes, required or not.
  • Use it for first-time guests, event sign-ups or prayer requests.
  • Put a form under a photo or video with the Spotlight block.
Photo and PDF viewer

Drop in the bulletin. DoorStep finds the links.

Any button can open a photo or PDF full screen. DoorStep reads the file, finds its web addresses, emails, phone numbers and QR codes, and turns them into buttons named after what they do.

  • “Email Pastor Mike”, “Give online” and “Register”, named from the words beside each link.
  • Reads scanned pages and phone photos too, even slightly tilted ones.
  • On a PDF, each page shows only its own buttons.
